Misalignment vs Overlapping Misalignment is characterized by column anchor breaks and asynchronous lateral drift without bounding box intersection; Overlapping is defined by positive pixel intrusion and sibling boundary collision. Both exhibit high raw displacement due to layout reflow. max_column_anchor_break_px (Misalignment), max_vertical_intrusion_px & max_intersection_area_px2 (Overlapping)
Misalignment vs Truncation Misalignment displaces controls laterally off-grid while preserving text visibility; Truncation clips text at rigid container edges or appends ellipsis. Both can be triggered by text length expansion. max_residual_disp_px (Misalignment), verified_ellipsis_count & clipping_elements_count (Truncation)
Misalignment vs Untranslation Misalignment shows large spatial displacement with fully localized text; Untranslation shows near-zero spatial displacement but heavy English lexical retention. None (Spatial vs Lexical orthogonality). max_column_dx_std (Misalignment), total_latin_words_preserved (Untranslation)
Misalignment vs Repeated hotkey Misalignment has rich structural geometric evidence; Repeated hotkey is visually unobservable via plain OCR. None. Structural displacement vs Win32 resource metadata.
Overlapping vs Truncation Overlapping penetrates into sibling control boundaries; Truncation is confined inside rigid container boundaries. Both result from text expansion exceeding allocated horizontal space. total_intersection_area_px2 (Overlapping), verified_ellipsis_count (Truncation)
Overlapping vs Untranslation Overlapping is geometric collision; Untranslation is lexical preservation. None. max_horizontal_intrusion_px (Overlapping), genuine_untranslated_strings_count (Untranslation)
Overlapping vs Repeated hotkey Overlapping is physical collision; Repeated hotkey is unobservable mnemonic conflict. None. Geometric intrusion vs Resource table parsing.
Truncation vs Untranslation Truncation expands and gets clipped; Untranslation stays in English and does not expand. Both involve text content. verified_ellipsis_count (Truncation), total_latin_words_preserved (Untranslation)
Truncation vs Repeated hotkey Truncation is visible boundary clipping/ellipsis; Repeated hotkey is unobservable via OCR. None. Ellipsis/clipping vs Resource table parsing.
Untranslation vs Repeated hotkey Untranslation retains user-facing English words; Repeated hotkey involves duplicate accelerator keys. Both are text/symbol level defects. Preserved Latin dictionary words vs Accelerator mnemonics.
